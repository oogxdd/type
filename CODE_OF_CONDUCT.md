# Code of Conduct

We want Type's community spaces to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Keep discussions relevant and protect other people's privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **noodzakelijkonline@gmail.com**. Include links and enough context to understand the concern, but do not send passwords, tokens, or unrelated personal data. Maintainers will review reports and may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
