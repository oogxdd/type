Try dragging a note or folder:
• Drop in the middle of a folder to move inside it.
• Drop near the top or bottom of a row to reorder.
• Drop on the footer to move to the root.

Open “Заметка на русском” to try Cyrillic input, “Long document” to scroll, or “1,000 sample notes” for a large tree.

Tag names are editable text. Use #tag at a paragraph's start, or ::: #tag and a closing ::: for a block. A hashtag in the middle of a sentence is ordinary text.

Frontmatter is kept outside the editor. The row menu “Copy .md with frontmatter” copies the complete synthetic file, including its hidden header.

Changes disappear when the demo closes.
